# Clarify Requirements

## Workflow

1. Identify the intended outcome, affected stakeholders, authoritative sources, and decision owners. Inspect only relevant contracts, workflow evidence, and constraints.
2. Describe actors, in-scope and excluded behavior, rules, dependencies, and quality attributes. Distinguish confirmed requirements, proposals, assumptions, and open decisions. Honor explicit technology constraints and distinguish them from candidate solutions.
3. Look for contradictions, missing failure behavior, and disagreements about compatibility or priority. Ask focused questions where an answer materially changes the outcome. Name the decision owner and impact of unresolved questions.
4. State observable acceptance criteria for agreed behavior, including relevant boundaries and negative cases. Record measurement conditions for quality targets and flag conditions that remain unknown.
5. Check that criteria trace to actual requirements and that proposed answers are clearly marked. Return confirmed scope, criteria, assumptions, exclusions, and blocking decisions.

Use the [requirements checklist](references/requirements-checklist.md) when requirements conflict, failure behavior is unclear, or criteria need a traceability check.

## Boundaries

Keep elicitation and analysis read-only. Preserve uncertainty and approval status. A durable specification, architecture decision, work plan, or implementation change has its own workflow and authorization.
