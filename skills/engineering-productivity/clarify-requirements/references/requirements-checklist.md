# Requirements Checklist

| Concern | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Outcome | Actor, objective, observable result, affected workflow. |
| Rules | Preconditions, invariants, permissions, exceptions. |
| Quality attributes | Measurable targets or explicit unknowns. |
| Boundaries | Included and excluded behavior, dependencies. |
| Acceptance | Given conditions, action, observable outcome. |

## Gotchas

- A requested technology may be a constraint or merely a proposed solution; clarify which.
- Avoid defining latency targets without workload and measurement conditions.
- A success-only scenario misses rejected input, repeated actions, and partial failure.
- Trace criteria to actual requirements rather than inventing features.
