# Root Cause Analysis

## Workflow

1. Define the observed failure, affected scope, and the question the analysis must answer.
2. Gather available evidence and construct an event or behavior sequence with uncertainty clearly marked.
3. Distinguish symptoms, triggering conditions, failure mechanisms, and contributing systemic factors.
4. Compare alternative explanations and seek evidence that could contradict each one.
5. Explain causal links and identify which are confirmed, inferred, or unknown.
6. Propose proportionate corrective and preventive actions tied to supported causes, with verification criteria.

## Constraints

- Do not assign blame or force a single root cause when multiple factors contribute.
- Do not invent timelines, operational facts, or causal certainty.
- Keep implementation fixes and incident-wide coordination in their respective workflows.
- Do not modify systems or records without authorization.

## Expected Output

Provide a causal analysis with evidence, causal chain, contributing factors, uncertainty, corrective and preventive actions, and measurable follow-up criteria.
